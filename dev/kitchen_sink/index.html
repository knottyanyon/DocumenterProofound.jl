Definition

Frobenius norm of \(A \in \mathbb{C}^{m \times n}\)

\[\|A\|_F \;=\; \left(\sum_{i,j} |a_{ij}|^2\right)^{1/2} \;=\; \sqrt{\operatorname{tr}(A^* A)}.\]

Equivalently the $\ell^2$-norm of $A$ flattened to a vector, or the $\ell^2$-norm of its singular values.

Theorem (with foldable proof and cross-reference)

Schmidt Decomposition of \(\ket{\psi}\)

For any $\ket{\psi} \in \mathcal{H}_A \otimes \mathcal{H}_B$ there exist orthonormal sets $\{\ket{u_i}\} \subset \mathcal{H}_A$, $\{\ket{v_i}\} \subset \mathcal{H}_B$ and strictly positive reals $\lambda_i$ with $\sum_i \lambda_i^2 = 1$ such that

\[\ket{\psi} = \sum_{i=1}^{r} \lambda_i \ket{u_i} \otimes \ket{v_i}.\]

The integer $r = \operatorname{Sch}(\psi)$ is the Schmidt rank (see Definition 1 for the singular-value connection).

Proof

Write $\ket{\psi} = \sum_{ij} C_{ij}\ket{i}\ket{j}$ and apply the SVD $C = U\Sigma V^\dagger$. Left/right singular vectors give $\ket{u_i}$, $\ket{v_i}$; singular values give $\lambda_i$. Unitarity of $U$, $V$ establishes orthonormality; $\|\psi\|^2 = 1$ forces $\sum_i \lambda_i^2 = 1$. $\square$

Lemma

\(\operatorname{Sch}(\psi) \leq \min(d_A, d_B)\)

The Schmidt rank is bounded by the smaller Hilbert-space dimension, since the coefficient matrix $C \in \mathbb{C}^{d_A \times d_B}$ (see proof of Theorem 1) has at most $\min(d_A, d_B)$ nonzero singular values.

Corollary

Eckart–Young best rank-\(\nu\) approximation

The truncated SVD $A_\nu = \sum_{j=1}^\nu \sigma_j u_j v_j^*$ minimises $\|A - B\|_F$ over all rank-$\nu$ matrices $B$, where $\|\cdot\|_F$ is Definition 1.

Proposition

Entanglement entropy of \(\ket{\psi}\)

With the Schmidt decomposition of Theorem 1,

\[S(\psi) = -\sum_{i=1}^r \lambda_i^2 \log \lambda_i^2.\]

\[S(\psi) = 0\]

iff $\ket{\psi}$ is a product state (i.e. Lemma 1 gives $r = 1$).

Remark (no title)

A rapidly decaying spectrum $\lambda_1 \gg \lambda_2 \gg \cdots$ signals low entanglement — exactly when MPS methods are efficient.

Admonition styles

Norm identities

\[\|A\|_2 = \sigma_1, \qquad \|A\|_F = \sqrt{\sigma_1^2 + \cdots + \sigma_r^2}.\]

Induced matrix norm

The smallest $C$ such that $\|Ax\|_{(m)} \leq C\|x\|_{(n)}$ for all $x$.

Exercise

Verify that the Frobenius norm is unitarily invariant.

Power iteration

Repeat $v \leftarrow Av / \|Av\|$ until convergence.
